Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding bookings, visas, and customization

How do I book a tour package with TravelGo? ▼

You can browse any tour package on our Destinations page and click "Book Now". Fill in your basic details, travel dates, and number of guests. Our travel coordinator will contact you to finalize flights, hotel options, and send your itinerary confirmation.

Can I customize the days or activities in a package? ▼

Yes, absolutely! All our packages can be tailored to your preferences. You can add extra days, upgrade to 5-star villas, include private sightseeing guides, or choose specific adventure excursions.

What documents and visas are required for international travel? ▼

You need a valid passport with at least 6 months of validity from your travel date. Depending on the destination (e.g., Bali, Dubai, Paris), our Chennai visa desk assists you with tourist visas, e-visas, or visa-on-arrival guidelines.

What is your cancellation and refund policy? ▼

We offer 100% free cancellation up to 14 days before departure for most packages. For cancellations made within 7-14 days, a nominal processing fee applies. Full details are provided in your personalized booking contract.
